# Noepedia Glossary

> **Current architecture precedence:** implementation and new experiments must follow [CURRENT_ARCHITECTURE.md](CURRENT_ARCHITECTURE.md), [SOCRATIC_DAIMONION.md](SOCRATIC_DAIMONION.md), and [DIRECTION.md](DIRECTION.md). Older metaphors in this document are historical/conceptual where they conflict with that boundary.

> **Status: living glossary.**
>
> These terms are working architectural vocabulary. They are meant to reduce ambiguity while the system is still being discovered. A term may later split, merge, or be renamed if the architecture demands it.

---

## Field

The **Field** is the addressable relational substrate.

It contains objects, predicates, networks, OPEN positions, meta-objects, provenance, versions, revisions, and other addressable structures.

The field itself should not be confused with the process that traverses or transforms it.

> **The field is structure. It does not compute by itself.**

---

## Layer / პლასტი

A **Layer** is a locally coherent relational structure with its own distinctions, reusable objects or actions, and characteristic operations.

A layer does not have to be only "above" or "below" another layer.

Layers may form:

- chains;
- branches;
- trees;
- directed graphs;
- or temporary coalitions.

A lower layer may support several upper layers at once.

Example:

~~~text
            → auditory meta-layer
sensory layer
            → visual meta-layer
            → proprioceptive meta-layer
~~~

---

## Relational Surrogate

A **Relational Surrogate** is Noepedia's current internal equivalent of the relations made reachable by contact with an external object or process.

It is not the external object itself and is not assumed complete.

Its resolution may differ across relation networks.

---

## Reconstruction Resolution

**Reconstruction Resolution** is the local degree of relational detail currently distinguished for an object within a particular network, context, or task.

One object may be high-resolution geometrically and low-resolution causally.

---

## Rendered Region

A **Rendered Region** is a sufficiently reconstructed part of an otherwise incomplete object that can be used temporarily as an internal equivalent inside a hybrid working scene.

---

## Equivalent Substitution

**Equivalent Substitution** is the temporary replacement of an observed or unknown part with a rendered internal candidate so that its compatibility with the larger observed structure can be tested.

The substitution is a test, not a declaration of identity.

---

## Part–Whole Constraint Propagation

**Part–Whole Constraint Propagation** is the bidirectional use of relations between a part and its containing whole:

~~~text
PART ⇄ WHOLE
~~~

The candidate part constrains the whole; the observed whole constrains the candidate part.

---

## Reciprocal Reconstruction

**Reciprocal Reconstruction** uses partially corresponding processes to constrain each other's unresolved regions.

Examples include assembly/disassembly, recognition/generation, diagnosis/repair, and analysis/synthesis.

---

## Operation-Duality Network

An **Operation-Duality Network** records correspondences among operations in reciprocal processes.

The correspondence need not be one-to-one or exact inverse.

---

## Actualization Cycle

The **Actualization Cycle** is:

~~~text
RECEPTION
→ DECOMPOSITION
→ RECONSTRUCTION
→ COMPARISON
→ MISMATCH
→ ACTUALIZATION
→ EXCLUSION
→ TEST
→ UPDATE
~~~

The cycle makes unresolved model–world difference active while allowing already matched structure to recede into background.

---

## Mismatch Field

A **Mismatch Field** is the structured residual difference between incoming evidence and the reconstruction generated by the current relational surrogate.

Mismatch is not the same as OPEN.

---

## Exclusion Cascade

An **Exclusion Cascade** is the progressive elimination of candidate causes, parameters, relations, or models that already fit the observation, shrinking the residual search space until direct testing becomes cheap.

---

## Representational Rank

**Representational Rank** is a working measure of how many distinctions in a model remain independently supported rather than being reconstructed redundantly from the same surviving constraints.

It is currently an engineering metaphor, not a fixed linear-algebraic definition.

---

## Axis Withdrawal

**Axis Withdrawal** is the deliberate or accidental loss of one discrimination lens, relation network, or evidence channel in order to observe how the remaining representation deforms.

Controlled axis withdrawal can be used as a self-diagnostic experiment.

---

## Reconstruction Degeneracy

**Reconstruction Degeneracy** occurs when several materially different external states collapse into the same or nearly the same internal reconstruction.

Degeneracy becomes dangerous when the lost alternatives are no longer preserved as OPEN.

---

## Diagnostic Independence

**Diagnostic Independence** is the requirement that a self-check not depend entirely on the same projection or lens family whose integrity it is testing.

It may be supplied by another lens, reciprocal process, lower layer, external measurement, provenance path, or delayed outcome.

---

## Epistemic Degradation Atlas

An **Epistemic Degradation Atlas** is a proposed map from controlled losses of distinctions to their characteristic representational deformations, false closures, prediction failures, and useful discriminating tests.

It diagnoses knowledge-system integrity, not a person's clinical state.

---

## Conceptual Descent Test

The **Conceptual Descent Test** asks whether a supposedly mature abstraction actually makes lower-level implementation, experiment, reconstruction, or control easier.

If conceptual OPEN birth has slowed, the system should descend.

If lower-layer work remains unexpectedly difficult, that friction is evidence to REOPEN the higher abstraction.

---

## Epistemic Maturity

**Epistemic Maturity** is the declining rate at which ordinary novel contact generates genuinely new structural OPENs in a domain.

It is local and cyclic, not a claim of permanent completion.

---

## Layer Closure

**Layer Closure** occurs when a layer can reconstruct, compare, and validate the structures it is responsible for without generating a remaining higher-order requirement that demands another meta-layer.

Closure remains reversible through REOPEN.

---

## Homoiconic Freedom Transfer

**Homoiconic Freedom Transfer** is the movement of unresolved structural freedom from a lower layer into a newly born meta-layer when that freedom can no longer be represented or closed honestly in the lower layer's own vocabulary.

---
## Meta-Layer / მეტა-პლასტი

A **Meta-Layer** is a layer formed from stable patterns, relations, or folded structures of another layer.

Its defining properties are:

1. it creates higher-order distinctions;
2. it can relate those distinctions horizontally;
3. it preserves a reversible path back to lower structure;
4. it remains answerable to lower operational reality.

A meta-layer is not a detached metaphysical world.

It is a higher-order organization inside the same homoiconic field.

---

## Layer Birth

**Layer Birth** is the creation of a new layer when the current architecture can no longer represent, compare, coordinate, or control a recurring structure efficiently enough.

A new layer should not be added merely because another abstraction can be imagined.

A useful working criterion is:

> **New layer = new stable discrimination + new operational leverage + reversible mapping to lower layers.**

If the new layer adds only a new name but no new discrimination or operational advantage, it is not yet justified as a distinct layer.

---

## Branch / Layer Branching

**Layer Branching** occurs when one lower layer gives rise to several upper layers specialized for different recurring structures.

For example:

~~~text
visual sensory layer
├─ motion layer
├─ edge / contour layer
├─ depth layer
├─ object identity layer
└─ spatial relation layer
~~~

This is allowed because the architecture is a graph, not a vertical stack.

---

## Homoiconic Space

A **Homoiconic Space** is a relational space in which structures used to describe the field may themselves become addressable objects inside the same field.

Rules, networks, transformations, revisions, meta-objects, and even relations among layers can therefore be represented using the same general relational material.

Homoiconicity does not require all computation to happen in the same physical way.

> **Representation may be homoiconic while execution is heterogeneous and parallel.**

---

## META_OBJECT

A **META_OBJECT** is a reversible handle for a lower relational structure.

It allows a larger lower structure to move through another working context as one addressable object without destroying the path back to its internal detail.

~~~text
lower relational structure
        ↓ fold
META_OBJECT
        ↓ move / compare / relate
new working context
        ↓ unfold
lower relational structure
~~~

---

## OPEN

**OPEN** marks a position that the current structure requires but does not yet know how to fill honestly.

OPEN is not an error to hide.

It is a registered unresolved boundary.

---

## OPEN_SPACE

An **OPEN_SPACE** is a structured region of unresolved relational possibility.

It can contain constrained alternatives, candidate predicates, unresolved oppositions, or a requirement generated by existing structure.

---

## Topology

A layer's **Topology** is its traversable relation-geometry.

It is the set of available paths among distinctions, objects, folded structures, analogies, oppositions, requirements, and OPEN_SPACES.

A higher layer may act as an accelerator because movement through a compressed topology can reach a relational position faster than slow lower-layer sensory-motor learning.

---

## Small Semiotic Model (SLM)

**SLM** means **Small Semiotic Model** in Noepedia.

It does **not** mean Small Language Model.

An SLM is a compact neural model trained to operate on semiotic structure rather than to converse in natural language. The Socratic Daimonion is the canonical SLM-type component inside the Noepedia/LSM.

---

## Socratic Daimonion

The **Socratic Daimonion** is the learned semiotic warehouse operator over the persistent Noepedia field.

It routes, places, retrieves, selects procedures, assembles minimal task-specific mega-graphs, preserves provenance, and returns OPEN / NO_RESULT / ESCALATE when the available structure is insufficient.

It is not an LLM, not a truth oracle, and not a private author of epistemic facts.

> **No reaction → no token.**

Support tokens must be attributable to source contact or explicit procedure results.

Repeated stable Daimonion operations may be compiled into deterministic algorithms.

---

## Daimonion Training Polygon

A controlled synthetic environment with known ground truth used to train semiotic placement, retrieval, procedure selection, OPEN handling, and stopping.

---

## Minimal Sufficient Garment

A **Minimal Sufficient Garment** is the smallest temporary set of relation-network cuts, procedures, and context needed for a particular task or deployed specialist.

It may be widened locally when the competence boundary is crossed.

---

## Deterministic Sterility

**Deterministic Sterility** is the requirement that a compiled procedure operate through explicit contracts, bounded inputs/outputs, isolation from unintended direct mutation, regression tests, and versioned scope.

It is an engineering property, not an anthropomorphic moral trait.

---

## Layer-Local Daimonion — CANDIDATE / RESEARCH

> This is not a requirement of the current minimal canonical architecture. After the 2026-10-06 REOPEN, the Daimonion implementation is provisional. A single SLM-type neural operator is one historical candidate, not the canonical fixed form.

A **Layer-Local Daimonion** is a historical/research proposal for a Daimonion process bound to the context of one layer.

A new layer may be born together with its own Daimonion context.

This gives the architecture local extensibility:

~~~text
Layer_n + Daimonion_n
~~~

Adding a new layer does not require redesigning every existing layer.

---

## Daimonion Instance

A **Daimonion Instance** is one active execution of Daimonion work in a particular context.

The same architectural rule-set may produce many concurrent instances.

An instance is not required to map one-to-one to one CPU core, GPU core, thread, or process.

It is a logical process that may be scheduled on available hardware.

---

## External Boundary / Internal Multiplicity

Noepedia presents a mediated external transaction boundary even when many Daimonion instances are active inside it.

~~~text
outside participant
       ↓
one accountable field boundary
       ↓
many layer-local Daimonion instances
       ↓
persistent field
~~~

External systems should not have to reproduce or coordinate Noepedia's internal process topology.

> **One boundary, many Daimonion processes.**

---

## Daimonion Coalition — DEFERRED / RESEARCH

A **Daimonion Coalition** is a historical/research proposal for a temporary group of Daimonion instances that share a working scene.

It must not be interpreted as co-authoring epistemic facts. Facts/tokens still require source contact or explicit procedure results.

For example, a dream, hypothesis, or reconstruction may be produced jointly by several layer-local processes.

~~~text
Daimonion_A
Daimonion_B
Daimonion_C
      ↓
shared temporary scene
      ↓
joint result
~~~

---

## Dream / Internal Dream Scene — CANDIDATE / RESEARCH

This term moved from DEFERRED to CANDIDATE on 2026-10-06. It is not yet a canonical mechanism or implementation requirement.

A **Dream** in the older architecture is a temporarily protected internal scene in which one or more Daimonion processes continue structured traversal while ordinary lower sensory-motor coupling is reduced.

A dream may be:

- local to one layer;
- shared across several layers;
- or, in the deferred multi-process hypothesis, assembled from contributions of several Daimonion instances.

This is a working architectural term, not a claim that the model already explains biological dreaming.

---

## Galvanic Break — DEFERRED / RESEARCH

This mechanism remains a separate research hypothesis and is not part of the canonical Daimonion definition.

A **Galvanic Break** is a temporary selective interruption of direct causal coupling.

~~~text
inherited causal path
        ↓
BREAK
        ↓
protected reconstruction
        ↓
goal / relation formation
        ↓
RECONNECT
~~~

The system remains physically causal.

What changes is which causal channels dominate the active scene.

---

## Reconnection

**Reconnection** is the controlled return from a protected higher-order scene to lower operational layers.

A newly formed goal or relation may then enter the lower layer as an exogenous task relative to that lower layer, while remaining endogenous to the whole agent.

---

## Upward Validation

**Upward Validation** asks whether a lower pattern legitimately belongs to the higher object, class, or relation assigned to it.

Typical question:

> Does this lower structure really justify the proposed meta-object?

---

## Downward Validation

**Downward Validation** asks whether a higher object or relation still preserves real lower-layer sensory, motor, evidential, or operational function.

Typical question:

> Does this higher structure still work when unfolded back into the world?

---

## Bidirectional Meta-Link

A **Bidirectional Meta-Link** is a relation between two layers that is not owned by either layer alone.

It should be tested from both directions.

~~~text
lower layer
   ↑     ↓
validate validate
   ↑     ↓
upper layer
~~~

A strong meta-link should survive both upward and downward validation.

---

## Delegation / Handoff

**Delegation** is the transfer of active work from one Daimonion instance to another.

A valid handoff should preserve enough context to continue honestly:

- current field position;
- active goal;
- already tested relations;
- unresolved OPEN points;
- permissions;
- provenance;
- current obligations;
- conditions for reopening.

The receiving Daimonion should not have to reconstruct the entire history from zero.

---

## Split / Spawn

**Split** or **Spawn** is the decomposition of one working problem into several independently executable Daimonion processes.

~~~text
Task T
├─ T1 → D1
├─ T2 → D2
└─ T3 → D3
~~~

The split is structural, not merely hardware-driven.

The architecture marks pieces as independently executable when their dependencies permit separation.

---

## Merge

**Merge** is the controlled reconstruction of several returned results into one working scene.

A merge should preserve:

- provenance;
- disagreement;
- unresolved dependencies;
- and the possibility that the pieces do not actually belong together.

Merge is not forced consensus.

---

## Parallelizable Process

A **Parallelizable Process** is a process whose dependency structure allows it to be treated as logically independent from another process over some interval.

This label belongs to the **architecture**, not to the current hardware.

A process may be marked parallelizable even if only one processor exists and the processes must currently execute one after another.

> **Logical parallelism must be preserved even under serial execution.**

This is important because hardware capacity may change later.

---

## Logical Core

A **Logical Core** is a scheduling identity for one independently executable process.

It does not mean a physical CPU or GPU core.

If two processes are independent, they should be represented as two logical cores even when a single physical processor time-slices them.

~~~text
logical core A ─┐
                ├─ one CPU today
logical core B ─┘

logical core A → CPU 0 tomorrow
logical core B → CPU 1 tomorrow
~~~

The architecture should therefore preserve parallel structure before parallel hardware is available.

---

## Absolute Parallelization Principle — DEFERRED / RESEARCH

This is a scheduling/architecture research proposal, not a requirement of the minimal Noepedia/LSM or provisional Daimonion/operator architecture.

The **Absolute Parallelization Principle** is the working rule:

> **Any process that is structurally independent should be represented as independent immediately, even if it is currently executed serially.**

Scheduling is then a separate problem.

The architecture first preserves the true dependency graph.

The scheduler later maps that graph onto:

- one processor;
- many CPUs;
- GPU workgroups;
- FPGA regions;
- distributed machines;
- or other execution substrates.

This prevents today's hardware limitation from becoming tomorrow's architectural limitation.

---

## Semantic Parallelism

**Semantic Parallelism** is parallelism discovered from the structure of the problem itself.

Instead of a programmer predeclaring every parallel loop, the system may detect that two or more working regions have no immediate dependency and may proceed independently.

This is the intended deeper mechanism behind Daimonion splitting.

---

## Scheduler

The **Scheduler** maps logical processes onto physical compute resources.

The scheduler should not redefine the semantic dependency graph.

It answers a later question:

> Given the already discovered independent processes, where and when should each one run?

---

## Coordination Cost

**Coordination Cost** is the overhead created by splitting work across processes.

It includes:

- context packaging;
- synchronization;
- merge work;
- provenance maintenance;
- communication;
- and conflict resolution.

A useful working criterion for spawning parallel work is:

~~~text
expected parallel gain > coordination cost
~~~

---

## Process Ownership

**Process Ownership** means that a Daimonion instance temporarily carries responsibility for a defined piece of work.

Ownership is transferable.

It is not absolute authority over the field.

---

## Static Structure / Active Process

A central architectural distinction:

~~~text
FIELD / LAYER / META_OBJECT / OPEN
        = structure

DAIMONION INSTANCE
        = process
~~~

This distinction should remain visible in implementation.

The field does not need to "think" by itself.

Processes traverse and transform the field.

---

## Endogenous Cause

An **Endogenous Cause** is a cause generated inside the whole agent or system.

In the layered freedom mechanism, a goal formed in a protected meta-scene is endogenous relative to the whole agent.

---

## Exogenous Task Relative to a Lower Layer

An **Exogenous Task Relative to a Lower Layer** is a goal that enters a lower operational subsystem from another internal layer rather than arising from the lower layer's immediate need-selection chain.

This is relative exogeneity, not a claim of causelessness.

---

## Quantitative Freedom

**Quantitative Freedom** is the increase in available distinctions, choices, and reusable actions produced by richer layers.

More pixels in the world-model can produce more possible action.

This does not by itself escape determinism.

---

## Qualitative Freedom

**Qualitative Freedom** is the working hypothesis that a layered causal system may gain a new kind of autonomy when it can:

1. interrupt an inherited causal channel;
2. preserve a protected higher-order scene;
3. traverse a different topology;
4. form a goal;
5. reconnect;
6. return that goal as a new cause for a lower layer.

---

## Working Rule About Layer Count

No fixed number of layers is assumed as an axiom.

A currently observed count, including the historical working image of "nine", should be treated as an empirical or introspective hypothesis.

A new layer should be born only when there is a functional demand for it.

Layer creation may stop when the additional operational leverage no longer justifies the maintenance and coordination cost.

---

## Compact Architectural Summary

~~~text
FIELD
  ↓
layers / branches / meta-objects / OPEN_SPACES
  ↓
layer-local Daimonion instances
  ↓
split / dream / coalition / validation
  ↓
logical parallel processes
  ↓
scheduler
  ↓
available physical hardware
  ↓
merge / reconnection
  ↓
field updated with provenance
~~~

> **Preserve the dependency graph first. Optimize the hardware mapping second.**
