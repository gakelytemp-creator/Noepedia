## Comparison Table

| Noepedia concept | Scientific neighbor | What overlaps | Important difference |
|---|---|---|---|
| Reconstruction followed by mismatch actualization | **Predictive coding** — Rao & Ballard (1999) | Higher-level predictions are compared with lower-level activity; residual error is propagated. | Noepedia uses an explicit addressable semiotic field, provenance, OPEN structures, and persistent revision. It does not claim the cortex literally stores SPO networks. |
| Bidirectional recognition / reconstruction | **Wake–sleep algorithm** — Hinton, Dayan, Frey & Neal (1995) | Bottom-up recognition and top-down generative reconstruction train each other. | Noepedia's reciprocal reconstruction is not restricted to neural probabilistic layers and may connect arbitrary processes, relations, assembly/disassembly steps, or domain structures. |
| Testing perception by generating a candidate world-model | **Analysis by synthesis / Bayesian vision** — Yuille & Kersten (2006) | Candidate internal structure is used to explain or reconstruct observations. | Noepedia emphasizes persistent explicit relation networks, partial rendered substitution, OPENs, provenance, and cross-domain reuse rather than only perceptual inference. |
| Hierarchical generative reconstruction and prediction error | **Predictive processing** — Clark (2013); related free-energy formulations by Friston (2010) | Hierarchical prediction, bidirectional processing, mismatch/error, model revision. | Noepedia does not adopt free-energy minimization or Bayesian probability as its universal objective. Mismatch may be represented symbolically, structurally, causally, geometrically, or procedurally. |
| A useful internal surrogate of the external system | **Good Regulator theorem** — Conant & Ashby (1970) | Effective regulation is linked to possessing a model structurally related to the regulated system. | Noepedia is not primarily a regulator and explicitly accepts uneven, partial, task-relative resolution rather than requiring a single complete isomorphism. |
| Layer-local adaptation, temporary independence, escape from recurrent bad regimes | **Ashby's ultrastability** — *Design for a Brain* (1952/1960) | Adaptation can require a second-order process; partial successes require temporary independence/local stability. | Noepedia adds explicit meta-layers, OPENs, Daimonion processes, goal formation, provenance, and knowledge-field operations. |
| Need for enough discriminative capacity to regulate complexity | **Law of Requisite Variety** — Ashby (1956) | A regulator must have sufficient variety to absorb/distinguish disturbances. | Noepedia treats new distinctions as potentially generating new layers and explicit relational structures; it is not only a control-theory regulator. |
| Long-duration higher-level action handles | **Options / hierarchical reinforcement learning** — Sutton, Precup & Singh (1999) | Higher-level reusable units can summarize many lower primitive actions. | Noepedia meta-objects are not only action policies; they may represent explanations, relations, analogies, evidence structures, or other folds. |
| Internal thought continuing under reduced perceptual coupling | **Perceptual decoupling / mind wandering** — Smallwood & Schooler (2015) | Internally generated cognition can become partially decoupled from current perceptual input. | Noepedia's galvanic break is an architectural hypothesis about selective causal coupling and goal formation, not a claim that mind wandering directly implements the same mechanism. |
| Dreaming as internally generated model activity | **Hobson & Friston (2012)** | Dreaming is discussed in relation to internally generated activity, predictive coding, and optimization of internal models. | Noepedia uses dreams only as a clue that partial decoupling is possible; it does not claim that dreaming proves free will or its layer architecture. |
| OPEN-driven exploration and choosing what to learn next | **Intrinsic motivation / developmental robotics** — Oudeyer & Kaplan (2007); Oudeyer, Kaplan & Hafner (2007) | Exploration can be driven by learning progress, novelty, uncertainty, or competence development. | Noepedia ties exploration to explicit unresolved relational structure, provenance, and field-generated REQUIREMENTS rather than a scalar intrinsic reward alone. |
| Seeking experiences expected to improve a world model | **Curious model-building control systems** — Schmidhuber (1991) | An agent actively seeks situations expected to improve prediction/model knowledge. | Noepedia can generate questions from explicit OPEN topology and can delegate external measurement without treating curiosity reward as the whole architecture. |
| Selecting the most informative unresolved cases | **Active learning** — Settles (2009 survey and earlier literature) | Query strategies focus scarce labeling/measurement effort where it reduces uncertainty most. | Noepedia's selection target may be a relation, layer, provenance debt, causal conflict, OPEN_SPACE, or domain experiment, not only an unlabeled training example. |
| Shrinking candidate space until direct enumeration is cheap | **Version-space reduction / query-by-committee and active learning** | Informative queries can eliminate large sets of hypotheses. | Noepedia's exclusion cascade may operate over heterogeneous relational and procedural parameters rather than one statistical hypothesis class. |
| Parameter estimation by residual/prediction error in dynamical systems | **Prediction-error system identification** — Ljung tradition; Forssell & Ljung (1999) | Models are judged and refined by discrepancies between predicted and observed behavior. | Noepedia generalizes mismatch beyond dynamical numerical models to semiotic, structural, causal, and part–whole relations. |
| Assembly ↔ disassembly reciprocal constraints | **Assembly/disassembly sequence and path planning** — Ghandi & Masehian (2015 review) | Assembly and disassembly are dual families of planning problems with shared geometric/precedence constraints. | Noepedia proposes a generic operation-duality network in which reciprocal processes constrain each other's OPENs; it is not limited to manufacturing. |
| Structured representation of complex systems and patterns | **Pattern theory** — Grenander & Miller (1994); Grenander (1994) | Complex systems can be represented through structured mathematical relations and transformed patterns. | Noepedia uses its own homoiconic addressable SPO-oriented field, OPEN structures, Daimonion processes, provenance, and staged consolidation. |
| Learning by information gain / epistemic value | **Active inference and epistemic value** — Friston et al. (2015) | Actions can be valued partly for reducing uncertainty and gaining information. | Noepedia does not derive its behavior from expected free energy and does not identify uncertainty reduction with its entire notion of value or freedom. |

## Where Noepedia Currently Differs Most

The strongest current differences are architectural rather than claims of having discovered previously unknown phenomena.

Noepedia combines, in one explicit persistent field:

- addressable objects and predicates;
- reversible meta-objects;
- OPEN and OPEN_SPACE as first-class unresolved structures;
- provenance and revision history;
- task-specific mega-graphs;
- layer-local Daimonion processes;
- internal/external Daimonion modes;
- explicit layer birth and closure;
- logical parallelism independent of hardware mapping;
- equivalent substitution of partially rendered regions;
- reciprocal reconstruction across paired processes;
- mismatch-driven actualization;
- and a separation between domain systems and persistent epistemic organization.

The literature above contains strong precedents for many individual pieces.

The research question is therefore not "did nobody ever think of prediction error, hierarchy, curiosity, generative models, cybernetic regulation, or assembly/disassembly duality?"

Those questions already have rich histories.

The useful question is:

> **Does this particular combination of explicit relational field, OPEN-driven work generation, layer-local Socratic process, reconstruction mismatch, reciprocal substitution, and auditable revision form a useful architecture?**

That must be answered experimentally, not rhetorically.
