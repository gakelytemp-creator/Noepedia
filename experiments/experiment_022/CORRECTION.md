# Experiment 022 — Later Clarification

Experiment 022's historical result remains unchanged:

NO_CONFIRMED_OFFSET_EFFECT

The experiment rejected one global signed offset k applied across the mixed transition population.

Later Experiments 026, 027, and 030 showed that this should not be read as rejecting every fixed temporal relation.

Specifically, later evidence found:

- LOAD_ON (0->1): immediate high-regime response under the frozen operational rule.
- LOAD_OFF (1->0): a strongly concentrated delayed low-regime response, median 42 rows in both Experiments 027 and 030.

Therefore the precise retrospective interpretation is:

REJECTED: one symmetric/global fixed-offset model for both transition directions.

NOT REJECTED: direction-specific fixed or tightly concentrated timing relations.

This clarification does not alter the preregistered Experiment 022 test, its data, or its historical NO_CONFIRMED_OFFSET_EFFECT result.
