# Academic Paper Track

> **Working status:** research-paper architecture, not yet a submission draft.
>
> This file defines the academic spine of the Noepedia project. Its purpose is to separate the testable architecture from public-facing rhetoric, preserve provenance, connect each mechanism to relevant literature, and make clear which claims are hypotheses, engineering expectations, or already demonstrated facts.

---

## Working Title

**Noepedia: A Low-Cost Multi-Layer Epistemic Architecture for Auditable Knowledge, Reconstruction, and Open-Edge Intelligence**

Alternative:

**From Reconstruction Mismatch to Layer Closure: An Explicit Epistemic Architecture for LLM–LSM Systems**

---

## Central Research Question

Can a persistent, addressable, low-cost relational field externalize stabilized knowledge, expose unresolved structure, generate useful next questions, and cooperate with flexible neural models so that expensive inference is increasingly concentrated at genuinely open edges?

---

## Main Contributions to Test

1. **Explicit relational field** rather than knowledge existing only in model weights or prose.
2. **OPEN / OPEN_SPACE** as first-class unresolved structure.
3. **Socratic Daimonion** as an auditable process rather than a hidden sovereign controller.
4. **Task-specific mega-graphs** rather than keeping the whole archive active.
5. **Reversible meta-layers / META_OBJECTS** for compression with recoverable lower structure.
6. **Relational surrogate reconstruction** with uneven local resolution.
7. **Equivalent substitution** and part–whole constraint propagation.
8. **Reciprocal reconstruction** across paired incomplete processes.
9. **Mismatch-driven actualization** and exclusion cascades.
10. **Layer birth, layer closure, and cyclic epistemic maturity.**
11. **Explicit lens hierarchies** as stabilized forms of repeated cognitive compression.
12. **LLM ↔ LSM division of labor:** flexible lens invention vs explicit stabilization and reuse.
13. **Structural exchange beyond text** as a future interface between intelligent agents.
14. **Controlled degradation / axis withdrawal** for self-diagnosis of representation integrity.
15. **Economic hypothesis:** settled knowledge can migrate from expensive neural inference toward cheaper explicit traversal and verification.

---

## Required Scientific Discipline

Every major claim should be marked as one of:

- **Observed in prototype**
- **Engineering hypothesis**
- **Conceptual mechanism**
- **Expected measurable effect**
- **Open research question**
- **External scientific precedent / neighbor**

Noepedia should never present similarity to prior theory as proof.

Independent rediscovery is provenance, not priority.

## Paper Structure

### 1. Motivation
Why externalized, revisable, auditable knowledge is still a technical problem despite large neural models.

### 2. Related Work
Predictive coding, generative reconstruction, cybernetics, Good Regulator theorem, ultrastability, active learning, intrinsic motivation, hierarchical reinforcement learning, system identification, pattern theory, assembly/disassembly planning.

### 3. Core Representation
Objects, predicates as objects, relation networks, provenance, versions, OPEN, OPEN_SPACE.

### 4. Active Processes
Daimonion instances, introvert/extrovert/coupled modes, mega-graphs, mediation boundary.

### 5. Meta-Layers and Lenses
Reversible folding, layer birth, layer closure, resolution, lens hierarchy.

### 6. Reconstruction and Actualization
Relational surrogate, mismatch field, equivalent substitution, reciprocal reconstruction, exclusion cascade.

### 7. LLM–LSM Cooperation
Flexible neural reasoning at open edges; stabilization into explicit lenses; structural exchange.

### 8. Self-Diagnosis
Controlled axis withdrawal, representational rank, reconstruction degeneracy, diagnostic independence.

### 9. Economic / Computational Hypothesis
Cost, latency, energy, settled-vs-open routing, escalation criteria.

### 10. Experimental Program
OpenPCB, AISocket, Everett Portal, controlled degradation tests, reconstruction benchmarks.

### 11. Failure Criteria
What would falsify the architecture or make it economically useless.

### 12. Discussion
Scope, safety, social access, limits, and relation to AGI-risk discourse.

## Minimum Experimental Evidence Before Strong Publication Claims

The first serious paper should ideally include:

- one end-to-end relational reconstruction task;
- one mismatch-driven actualization case;
- one OPEN-generated next-measurement case;
- one equivalent-substitution or reciprocal-reconstruction case;
- one controlled degradation / self-detection case;
- one cost comparison between repeated LLM reconstruction and stabilized LSM traversal;
- one case where the architecture fails and REOPEN is required.

---

## Safety and AGI Framing

The paper should avoid the claim:

> "Noepedia solves AGI safety."

The defensible claim is narrower:

> Noepedia proposes an auditable epistemic architecture that may reduce several classes of opacity, repeated inference, unsupported completion, concentration of expensive cognition, and uninspectable goal formation.

The AGI-safety relevance must be argued mechanism by mechanism.
